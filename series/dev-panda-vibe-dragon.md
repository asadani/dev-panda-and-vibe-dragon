## Promise and world

A comic about two developers trying to ship software while AI keeps moving the goalposts. They maintain a small software product together, dealing with users, deadlines, bills, and incidents. The product's name and technical stack are intentionally unspecified until an episode establishes them.

Dev Panda is a curious junior who wants to build something useful and earn respect. He experiments, questions inherited assumptions, and sometimes confuses a working demo with production readiness. He is competent and can be right.

Vibe Dragon is an experienced principal who wants to protect the product and Panda. Dry humour, incident experience, and systems judgment coexist with blind spots: overengineering and mistaking past experience for a universal law. Dragon is opinionated but ultimately kind.

Their relationship includes mutual affection, shared responsibility, and occasional joint mistakes. Show kindness through action. Avoid making every episode a junior's error followed by a senior's lecture. Give situations consequences and visual endings.
